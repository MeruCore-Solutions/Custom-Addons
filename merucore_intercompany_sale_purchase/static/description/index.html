MeruCore Intercompany Sale Purchase Sync

Synchronize Sales Orders and Purchase Orders across two companies in the same Odoo database with one central control-center transaction.

  • SO to PO and PO to SO creation
  • Controlled bidirectional supported-field synchronization
  • Idempotency, recursion prevention, and retry handling
  • Product, UoM, pricing, currency, and tax strategies
  • Conflict preview and immutable audit events
  • Multi-company isolation aligned with the base control center

Limitations: same database only. Stock, receipts, deliveries, invoices, bills, payments, and accounting automation require separate extensions.
